The Wren logo and app icon — the chat bird in assets/logo.png. Use it for the home-screen icon, the splash, and once in the app: 44px, top-left on Welcome. It is the only illustration Wren has: there is no mascot.

```jsx
<Logo size={96} />     // brand mark
<Logo size={64} />     // compact lockup
```

- Flat. Do not recolor, outline, shadow, or crop the crest, beak, or tail.
- On the app icon, sit it on white with clear space so the iOS rounded mask does not clip it.
