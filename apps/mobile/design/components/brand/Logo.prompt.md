The Wren logo and app icon — the chat bird in assets/logo.png. Use it for the home-screen icon, splash, and any small brand mark. The round bird (`Wren`) is the mascot and stays on Home, Someone, Amount, and Sent.

```jsx
<Logo size={96} />     // brand mark
<Logo size={64} />     // compact lockup
```

- Flat. Do not recolor, outline, shadow, or crop the crest, beak, or tail.
- On the app icon, sit it on white with clear space so the iOS rounded mask does not clip it.
- The mascot and the logo are different drawings. Do not swap them.
