Pill button; the primary variant is the single electric-blue action on a screen.

```jsx
<Button full>Send</Button>
<Button variant="secondary" size="md">Cancel</Button>
```

- Pinned actions: `size="lg" full`, 24px side margins, 34px above the home indicator.
- Press: scales to 0.97 and darkens to --action-pressed. No hover glow, no shadow.
- Never rectangular. Never two primaries on one screen.
